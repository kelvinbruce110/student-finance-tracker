      About PennyWise

      A simple, private expense tracker for students.

      Purpose

      PennyWise helps students track their finances. Log what you spend, see where it goes, search your history with regular expressions and stay under a monthly cap. Everything runs in your browser and your data never leaves your device.

      Good to know

      • Amounts are stored in Rwandan francs (RWF). USD and EUR use the rates you set in Settings.
      • Press ? anywhere to see the keyboard shortcuts.
      • The search box accepts real regular expressions, like (coffee|tea).
